Telltale

Three scenes. Twenty-four small lies. Find them all.

Two pictures look the same. They aren't. Tap a difference on either picture to circle it — on both. A wrong tap costs five seconds, so look twice and tap once.

How to play

  • Click a difference on either picture — 100 points, more on a streak.
  • Wrong click: −5 seconds and your streak resets.
  • Hint (3 per level): a gold ring points at one difference, −50 points.
  • Finish fast: time left becomes bonus points and stars.
  • Keys: H hint · R restart · Esc pause · M sound
